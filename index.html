How it all began

Your moments are
our favourite occasions.

A&A Events was founded by Dr. Ashley Nyemba-Ahmed, a medical doctor and caterer, and Ms. Amanda Nyemba LLB, a legal professional and events organiser.

A&A Events began around a family table, with the belief that the most memorable gatherings aren't only about what's on the plate—they're about how people feel.

Today, our team brings that same warmth to weddings, corporate events, birthdays and intimate celebrations. We listen closely, cook thoughtfully and handle every detail so you can be fully present.
